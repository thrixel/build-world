# Game plan

## Orrery Lock

Genre: environmental puzzle adventure.

The player explores an observatory, retrieves three star cells, installs them in a console, and turns the orrery crank. The independently rotating rings align, the vault opens, and the player reaches the star prism.

Pipeline proof:

- multi-part Architect asset with independently moving rings and crank;
- pivot preservation through grouping, export, and Roblox import;
- PBR metals, glass, and emissive accents;
- collision and interaction around a hero prop;
- third-person desktop/mobile play.

## Moonmarket Mix-Up

Genre: fixed-isometric arcade collection game.

The player fulfills timed potion orders by gathering moonfruit, glowcap, and star crystal ingredients from a compact bazaar and delivering the correct combination to the cauldron.

Pipeline proof:

- cohesive family of static organic and constructed props;
- style-reference reuse;
- fixed-camera readability and mobile controls;
- runtime pickup respawning, effects, and performance under repetition;
- a game loop structurally unrelated to Orrery Lock.

## Resource plan

The build allocated 1,250 Thrixel Cubes to the primary asset pass and held 4,000 in reserve for
corrective passes and final evidence work. The completed asset set used 550 of the available 5,250
Cubes and required no additional purchases.
